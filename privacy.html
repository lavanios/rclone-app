Privacy Policy

App: rclone (personal backup client) · Owner: Danylo Lavunov

This Google Cloud OAuth client is used only by its owner to back up personal video files from their own computer to their own Google Drive using rclone.

  • The app requests access only to files it creates in Google Drive (drive.file scope).
  • No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, shared, or sold. Files go directly from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
